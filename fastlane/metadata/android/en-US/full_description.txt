Cassette plays the music you own.

Choose records from the music on your own computer, and Cassette carries them to your phone over Wi-Fi. From then on they play straight from the phone, with or without signal, on the lock screen and in Android Auto.

It also tunes in to Cassette's dial of real radio stations.

You'll need
• A Cassette account. Cassette is in beta: ask for a spot at www.cassette.digital.
• The Cassette app on your computer, where your music lives. Until it's set up, the radio still plays.

Open source
Cassette for Android is a modified version of Tempo, the open-source music player by Antonio Cappiello and its contributors. Its code is public at github.com/cassettedigital/cassette-android under the GPL v3. Thank you to the Tempo project for the foundation.
